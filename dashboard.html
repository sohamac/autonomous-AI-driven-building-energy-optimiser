Selection rule: best energy savings among setpoints keeping comfort violations within 20% of baseline (38 × 1.2 = 45.6 max). 25.0°C is the highest-savings point that still qualifies.

Methodology

Search: deterministic sweep of the building-wide cooling setpoint schedule (heating fixed), each point evaluated with a full annual EnergyPlus 26.1.0 simulation against Mumbai ISHRAE weather data.

Why not the LLM agent's own choice: an autonomous LLM agent (llama-3.1-8b-instant via Groq, MCP-style tool-calling) was given the same tools and explicit trend-feedback verdicts ("GOT WORSE — reverse direction") but made three consecutive setpoint changes that worsened both energy and comfort, ignoring its own verdict data. The deterministic sweep replaces the agent's final decision-making step with a transparent, inspectable rule, while the agent's tool-calling loop and MCP architecture remain part of the demonstrated system.

Constraint: PMV thermal comfort band -0.5 to +0.5, violation = >10% of occupied hours outside that band in a given month/zone.
